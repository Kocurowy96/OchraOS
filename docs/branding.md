# Branding

## Colours

- **Primary:** Pine `#047857` — logo, mascots, default Material seed colour.
- **Secondary:** Ochre `#CC7722` — accents in the logo and in the mascots' fur.
- **Font:** Roboto Flex (OFL).

**Accent colours** (picked in Settings, palette generated from the seed):
Blurple `#635BFF`, Sky `#0EA5E9`, Violet `#7C3AED`, Midnight `#0F172A`, Lagoon `#0891B2`,
Olive `#65A30D`, Sunflower `#FACC15`, Amber `#D97706`, Red `#DC2626`, Deep teal `#0D9488`,
Lavender `#8B5CF6`, Royal blue `#2563EB`, Indigo `#4F46E5`.
Midnight and Sunflower need manual contrast checks.

## Mascots

Three cats based on the owner's cats, each with an ochre accent.

| Mascot | Breed | Accent | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bursztyn | ginger Maine Coon | ochre fur, pine collar | face of the system: logo, installer, Ochra Welcome |
| Selenit | white Devon Rex | ochre ear tips or collar | developers: Nightly, Beta, SDK, crash screens |
| Tanzanit "Tazi" | blue British Longhair with a white bib | copper-ochre eyes | Ochra Guard: permission prompts, lock screen |

- Style: **chibi** (big heads and eyes, soft shapes, blush), chosen from three concept directions
  (geometric, chibi, line and colour blob) on 2026-11-22.
- Source artwork is vector (SVG, made in Affinity); PNG exported where needed (bootloader,
  GitHub avatar, thumbnails). Icons at 16–32 px are hand-tuned.
- Artwork licence: CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Sound

- First boot after installation: a longer welcome theme.
- Every boot: a short 2–3 s motif, can be turned off.
- Both built from the same 3–4 note motif, reused in other system sounds.
